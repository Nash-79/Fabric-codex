# Search

The **Search** page covers the whole knowledge base from one box. Results update as you
type and group into:

- **Articles** — topic articles (title, summary, and full body text are indexed).
- **Topics** — names and descriptions in the topic tree.
- **Claims** — the atomic verified/pending facts themselves.
- **Sources** — titles and reader summaries of ingested sources.

## Narrowing results

- The **kind chips** (All / Articles / Topics / Claims / Sources) limit results to one group.
- Click any **#tag** on a result to filter by that hashtag; click it again (×) to clear.
- Matched words are highlighted in each result's snippet.

Clicking a result takes you to the right place: articles open in the reader, topics open in
the tree, claims open the Registry filtered to their capability, sources open the Sources
page.

## What search reflects

Search always reflects the current state of the knowledge base: superseded article versions
and deprecated claims never appear, and a claim's status shown in results is live, not a
stale copy. Only active content is searchable.
